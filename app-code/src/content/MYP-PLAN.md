## MYP conventions
- **British/international English** as in IB documents: colour, centre, metre, litre, analyse, organise, behaviour, sulfur (IUPAC), aluminium. "Mathematics" in titles, "maths" in prose is fine. No emojis.
- SI units and metric throughout; money uses international settings with the currency written as a word or code, never a `$` sign (e.g. "45 euros", "USD 12.50", "CHF 8.20"). Use `$g = 9.8\ \text{N/kg}$` (MYP 1-3 may say "use $g = 10\ \text{N/kg}$" when stated in the question).
- Ages: MYP 1 is 11-12, MYP 2 12-13, MYP 3 13-14, MYP 4 14-15, MYP 5 15-16 (eAssessment level). Pitch MYP 1-3 like a rigorous lower-secondary course; MYP 5 like the MYP eAssessment on-screen exams.
- **MYP assessment criteria** go in `standards` (2-4 tags per lesson), alongside the framework strand:
  - Mathematics: `"Criterion A: Knowing and understanding"`, `"Criterion B: Investigating patterns"`, `"Criterion C: Communicating"`, `"Criterion D: Applying mathematics in real-life contexts"`, plus the branch, e.g. `"MYP Maths: Number"`, `"MYP Maths: Algebra"`, `"MYP Maths: Geometry and trigonometry"`, `"MYP Maths: Statistics and probability"`.
  - Sciences: `"Criterion A: Knowing and understanding"`, `"Criterion B: Inquiring and designing"`, `"Criterion C: Processing and evaluating"`, `"Criterion D: Reflecting on the impacts of science"`, plus one `"Key concept: ..."` (Change, Relationships, Systems) and optionally `"Related concept: ..."` (e.g. Energy, Form, Function, Interaction, Models, Movement, Patterns, Transformation, Evidence, Balance, Consequences, Environment).
  - Optionally one `"Global context: ..."` (Identities and relationships, Orientation in space and time, Personal and cultural expression, Scientific and technical innovation, Globalization and sustainability, Fairness and development).
- Every lesson should include at least one question in the style of Criterion B (Maths: find and generalise a pattern, justify a rule; Sciences: hypothesis with scientific reasoning, variables, method design) or Criterion D (real-life application or evaluating impacts). Science q4 structured questions often mix A, B/C and D.
- Mark codes remain B/M/A/R as in the base specs (the MYP 0-8 criterion levels are not used for marking individual questions).

## Unit plan, MYP 1

### myp-maths-y1 MYP 1 Mathematics (3 units)
- unit-1 "Number": 1-1 Integers and Order of Operations (a) place value, ordering and rounding integers and decimals, order of operations with brackets (b) adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing negative numbers, directed numbers in context (temperature, bank balances); 1-2 Factors, Multiples and Primes (a) prime factorisation with factor trees and index form, HCF and LCM including word problems (b) squares, cubes, square and cube roots, divisibility rules, investigating patterns in square numbers (Criterion B); 1-3 Fractions, Decimals and Percentages (a) equivalent fractions, adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing fractions and mixed numbers (b) converting between fractions, decimals and percentages, percentage of a quantity, simple percentage increase and decrease
- unit-2 "Algebra": 2-1 Algebraic Expressions (a) variables, writing expressions from words, collecting like terms, simple expansion of one bracket (b) substitution into expressions and formulae, including negative values; 2-2 Linear Equations (a) solving one- and two-step equations, checking solutions (b) forming and solving equations from real-life problems and geometry (Criterion D); 2-3 Patterns and Ratio (a) number sequences, term-to-term and position-to-term rules, finding and justifying the nth term of linear sequences from patterns of shapes (Criterion B) (b) ratio notation, simplifying ratios, sharing in a ratio, simple direct proportion and best buys
- unit-3 "Geometry, Measurement and Data": 3-1 Angles and Shapes (a) angle facts: on a line, at a point, vertically opposite, angles in triangles and quadrilaterals, with reasons (b) properties of triangles, quadrilaterals and polygons, interior angle sum of polygons as a pattern (Criterion B); 3-2 Measurement (a) perimeter and area of rectangles, triangles, parallelograms, trapezia and compound shapes (b) volume and surface area of cuboids, converting metric units of length, area, capacity and mass; 3-3 Statistics and Probability (a) mean, median, mode and range, choosing an average, reading and drawing bar charts and pictograms described in words (b) the probability scale, theoretical probability of single events, listing outcomes, experimental probability and relative frequency

### myp-sciences-y1 MYP 1 Sciences (3 units, integrated)
- unit-1 "Being a Scientist and Matter": 1-1 Scientific Inquiry (a) laboratory safety and equipment, hazard symbols, independent, dependent and control variables, writing a testable question and hypothesis with reasoning (Criterion B) (b) measuring with correct units and precision, recording data in tables, drawing line graphs described in words, identifying trends and anomalies (Criterion C); 1-2 States of Matter (a) the particle model of solids, liquids and gases, explaining properties (b) changes of state, melting and boiling points, heating and cooling curves, evaporation vs boiling, diffusion; 1-3 Mixtures and Separation (a) elements, compounds and mixtures in simple terms, pure substances, solutions, solute, solvent, solubility and saturated solutions (b) filtration, evaporation, crystallisation, simple distillation and paper chromatography with $R_f$ values, choosing a method
- unit-2 "Living Things": 2-1 Cells (a) characteristics of living things (MRS GREN), animal and plant cell structures and their functions, specialised cells (b) using a light microscope, calculating magnification $\text{magnification} = \frac{\text{image size}}{\text{actual size}}$ with unit conversions (mm to µm); 2-2 Classification and Adaptation (a) classification into kingdoms, vertebrate and invertebrate groups, using and constructing dichotomous keys (b) variation (continuous and discontinuous) and adaptations of organisms to their habitats; 2-3 Ecosystems (a) food chains and food webs, producers, consumers and decomposers, predicting effects of changes in a food web (Key concept: Systems) (b) habitats, interdependence, human impact on ecosystems such as deforestation and plastic pollution, evaluating solutions (Criterion D)
- unit-3 "Forces, Energy and Space": 3-1 Forces (a) types of force (contact and non-contact), measuring in newtons with a newton meter, force diagrams described in words, balanced and unbalanced forces and resultant force in one dimension (b) mass vs weight $W = mg$, friction and air resistance, investigating friction (Criterion B); 3-2 Energy (a) energy stores and transfers, conservation of energy, simple energy transfer diagrams described in words, efficiency in simple terms (b) renewable and non-renewable energy resources, evaluating their advantages and impacts (Criterion D, Global context: Globalization and sustainability); 3-3 Earth and Space (a) the solar system, day and night, years, seasons from the tilt of Earth's axis (b) phases of the Moon, eclipses, gravity and orbits in simple terms

## Unit plan, MYP 2
Ages 12-13. Build on MYP 1; do not repeat MYP 1 topics at the same depth (extend them).

### myp-maths-y2 MYP 2 Mathematics (3 units, prefix `mypm2-uN-`)
- unit-1 "Number and Proportion": 1-1 Powers, Roots and Standard Form (a) index notation, laws of indices for multiplying and dividing with positive integer powers, power of zero (b) standard form for large and small numbers, converting and calculating, ordering, real-life contexts (distances in space, cell sizes) (Criterion D); 1-2 Percentages and Finance (a) percentage change, reverse percentages, expressing one quantity as a percentage of another (b) simple interest, compound growth year by year, discounts, tax and profit/loss with currencies written as words or codes (Criterion D); 1-3 Ratio, Rates and Proportion (a) direct and inverse proportion, unitary method, recipe and map-scale problems (b) compound measures: speed, density and unit rates, converting units of speed, exchange rates
- unit-2 "Algebra and Graphs": 2-1 Expanding and Factorising (a) expanding single brackets and simplifying, expanding a product of two binomials $(x+a)(x+b)$ (b) factorising by common factors, investigating and justifying algebraic identities from number patterns (Criterion B); 2-2 Equations and Inequalities (a) equations with unknowns on both sides, with brackets, and with simple fractions (b) linear inequalities, solving and representing on a number line described in words, integer solutions, forming from contexts; 2-3 Coordinates and Linear Graphs (a) coordinates in four quadrants, midpoint, plotting $y = mx + c$ from a table of values, horizontal and vertical lines (b) gradient as rate of change, interpreting gradient and intercept in real-life graphs (distance-time, cost), finding the equation of a line from two points
- unit-3 "Geometry and Statistics": 3-1 Circles and Area (a) parts of a circle, circumference and area of circles and semicircles, giving answers in terms of $\pi$ (b) arc length and sector area as fractions of a circle, compound shapes with circles, volume of prisms and cylinders; 3-2 Transformations and Constructions (a) reflection, rotation, translation (column vectors) and enlargement with scale factor, described in words and coordinates (b) symmetry, congruence and similarity in simple terms, bearings and scale drawings, angles in parallel lines (alternate, corresponding, co-interior) with reasons; 3-3 Data Handling and Probability (a) grouped frequency tables, estimated mean, modal class, comparing two distributions using average and range, scatter graphs and correlation described in words (b) sample space diagrams for two events, probability of events not happening, mutually exclusive events, expected outcomes, two-way tables and Venn diagrams with set notation basics

### myp-sciences-y2 MYP 2 Sciences (3 units, integrated, prefix `myps2-uN-`)
- unit-1 "Atoms and Reactions": 1-1 Atoms, Elements and the Periodic Table (a) atoms, simple atomic structure (protons, neutrons, electrons), element symbols, the periodic table as groups and periods, metals and non-metals (b) molecules and compounds, chemical formulae and counting atoms, word equations; 1-2 Chemical Reactions (a) physical vs chemical change, signs of a chemical reaction, conservation of mass with calculations (b) combustion, oxidation, thermal decomposition, exothermic and endothermic reactions with temperature-change data (Criterion C); 1-3 Acids and Alkalis (a) the pH scale, indicators (litmus, universal indicator), strong and weak in simple terms (b) neutralisation word equations, salts, everyday uses (indigestion, soil treatment), investigating which antacid neutralises most acid (Criterion B)
- unit-2 "The Human Body": 2-1 Nutrition and Digestion (a) the food groups and a balanced diet, energy in food, deficiency diseases, food tests (iodine, Benedict's, biuret, ethanol emulsion) (b) the digestive system, enzymes as biological catalysts in simple terms, absorption in the small intestine, investigating enzyme activity and temperature (Criterion B); 2-2 Breathing and Circulation (a) the respiratory system, gas exchange in the alveoli, effects of smoking (Criterion D) (b) the heart, blood vessels and blood, aerobic respiration word equation, effect of exercise on heart rate with data (Criterion C); 2-3 Reproduction and Health (a) human reproductive systems, puberty, fertilisation and development in simple scientific terms, the menstrual cycle at an introductory level (b) microorganisms and disease, the immune system basics, vaccination and antibiotics, evaluating public health measures (Criterion D, Global context: Fairness and development)
- unit-3 "Waves and Electricity": 3-1 Sound and Light (a) sound as vibrations, need for a medium, frequency, amplitude and pitch/loudness, speed of sound and echoes with $\text{speed} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$ (b) light travelling in straight lines, reflection (angle of incidence = angle of reflection), refraction in simple terms, the visible spectrum and colour filters; 3-2 Electricity (a) circuits described in words, current measured with ammeters in amps, potential difference with voltmeters in volts, series vs parallel circuit rules (b) resistance $V = IR$ in simple calculations, conductors and insulators, electrical safety and energy cost in kWh with currency written as words or codes (Criterion D); 3-3 Magnetism and Motion (a) magnets, magnetic fields described in words, electromagnets and investigating factors affecting their strength (Criterion B) (b) speed, distance-time graphs described in words, average speed calculations, pressure $p = \frac{F}{A}$ in simple contexts

## Unit plan, MYP 3
Ages 13-14. Build on MYP 2; do not repeat MYP 1-2 topics at the same depth (extend them).

### myp-maths-y3 MYP 3 Mathematics (3 units, prefix `mypm3-uN-`)
- unit-1 "Number": 1-1 Number Systems (a) rational and irrational numbers, terminating vs recurring decimals, ordering and rounding extended (b) absolute value, order of operations with negatives, fractions and multiple levels of brackets; 1-2 Powers, Roots and Standard Form (a) laws of indices: products and quotients of powers, power of a power, roots as fractional powers only in words (b) standard form calculations including addition and subtraction of numbers in standard form, orders of magnitude and real-life contexts (Criterion D); 1-3 Percentages, Finance and Proportion (a) successive percentage change, reverse percentages, percentage of a percentage (b) direct and inverse proportion, compound measures extended, exchange rates and best-buy problems with currencies written as words or codes (Criterion D)
- unit-2 "Algebra": 2-1 Expanding and Factorising (a) expanding single brackets and products of two binomials $(x+a)(x+b)$, $(x+a)^2$, simplifying (b) factorising by common factors and factorising monic quadratics $x^2+bx+c$ by inspection; 2-2 Equations and Inequalities (a) linear equations with unknowns on both sides, with brackets and with fractions (b) linear inequalities, compound (double) inequalities, representing solution sets on a number line described in words, forming inequalities from contexts; 2-3 Sequences and Patterns (a) the $n$th term of linear and simple quadratic sequences from tables and patterns of shapes (Criterion B) (b) generating and justifying rules, solving problems involving sequences in context
- unit-3 "Geometry, Trigonometry and Data": 3-1 Pythagoras and Right-Angled Trigonometry (a) Pythagoras' theorem in 2D and 3D problems, exact surd answers where appropriate (b) sine, cosine and tangent ratios in right-angled triangles, finding sides and angles, bearings applications (Criterion D); 3-2 Measurement Extended (a) area of triangles, parallelograms and trapezia using perpendicular heights including trigonometric heights, compound areas (b) volume and surface area of prisms, cylinders and composite solids, metric conversions in multi-step problems; 3-3 Statistics and Probability Extended (a) scatter graphs with correlation described in words, using lines of best fit to estimate and discuss reliability (b) tree diagrams for combined events, mutually exclusive vs independent events, theoretical vs experimental probability, expected outcomes in context

### myp-sciences-y3 MYP 3 Sciences (3 units, integrated, prefix `myps3-uN-`)
- unit-1 "Atoms, Reactions and Materials": 1-1 Atomic Structure and the Periodic Table (a) protons, neutrons and electrons, atomic number and mass number, electron shells and arrangement (Key concept: Systems) (b) groups and periods, metals and non-metals, reactivity trends, ions formed by electron transfer; 1-2 Chemical Reactions (a) word and symbol equations, balancing equations, types of reaction: synthesis, decomposition, displacement and combustion (b) energy changes in reactions: exothermic and endothermic profiles described in words, conservation of mass calculations extended (Criterion C); 1-3 Acids, Alkalis and Salts (a) the pH scale and indicators extended, acid reactions with metals and carbonates, neutralisation word equations (b) making soluble salts by titration and by precipitation, precipitation reactions, choosing and evaluating a method (Criterion B)
- unit-2 "Living Things Extended": 2-1 Classification and Change (a) classification systems and major groups, constructing and using dichotomous keys, continuous vs discontinuous variation extended (b) natural selection and adaptation as explanations of how species change, evaluating evidence (Key concept: Change, Criterion D); 2-2 Photosynthesis and Respiration (a) word equations for photosynthesis and aerobic respiration, inputs, outputs and the role of chlorophyll and light (b) comparing photosynthesis and respiration, gas exchange in leaves and alveoli, limiting factors in simple terms; 2-3 Human Body Systems (a) the organ systems: digestive, circulatory, respiratory, nervous and excretory systems and their interactions (Key concept: Relationships) (b) homeostasis and negative feedback in simple terms, evaluating the impacts of lifestyle and disease on body systems (Criterion D)
- unit-3 "Forces, Energy and Earth": 3-1 Motion and Forces (a) speed, velocity and acceleration, distance-time and velocity-time graphs described in words, interpreting gradients (b) resultant forces extended, turning effects of forces (moments) in simple balanced/unbalanced investigations (Criterion B); 3-2 Energy and Waves (a) energy stores, transfers and efficiency calculations, thermal energy and the three methods of heat transfer: conduction, convection, radiation (b) the electromagnetic spectrum and uses of different waves, the wave relationship $v = f\lambda$, ultrasound and other wave applications (Criterion D); 3-3 Electricity and Earth Science (a) Ohm's law $V = IR$ applied to series and parallel circuits, power $P = VI$, resistors in circuits (b) the structure of the Earth, rocks and the rock cycle, weathering and erosion, climate vs weather (Key concept: Systems)

## Unit plan, MYP 4-5
MYP 4 and 5 use the MYP 4-5 framework: Mathematics splits into Standard and Extended courses; Sciences split into Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Each year has the same three-unit shape per course; Year 5 deepens and extends Year 4. Official strands: mathematics = number, algebra, geometry and trigonometry, statistics and probability, discrete mathematics; sciences follow each subject's topic areas with key concepts Change, Relationships, Systems and related concepts.

### myp-maths-std-y4 / myp-maths-ext-y4 / myp-maths-std-y5 / myp-maths-ext-y5
Prefixes: `mypms4-`, `mypme4-`, `mypms5-`, `mypme5-`. MYP 4-5 mathematics uses the five branches: number; algebra; geometry and trigonometry; statistics and probability; discrete mathematics. Extended includes all Standard content plus additional depth (fractional indices, logarithms, function transformations, sequences formulae, vectors intro). Three units per course:
- unit-1 "Number and Algebra Foundations": absolute value and inequalities (compound and double), irrational numbers and surds, standard form extended, laws of indices with integer and negative powers, number systems notation, direct and inverse proportion, number sequences; Extended adds: fractional/rational indices, logarithms and their laws, upper and lower bounds.
- unit-2 "Functions": mappings and function notation, domain and range, linear functions $y = mx + c$, parallel and perpendicular lines, systems of equations, quadratic functions and completing the square intro; Extended adds: transformations of functions, rational and trigonometric graphs, composite functions.
- unit-3 "Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics and Probability": metric conversions, volume of regular polyhedra, similarity and congruence, coordinate geometry (distance, midpoint, gradient), transformations and tessellations, circle geometry extended, right-angled and non-right-angled trigonometry with the sine and cosine rules; statistics: measures of spread, cumulative frequency, scatter with regression line, probability of combined events, expected value, sample space diagrams; Extended adds: vectors in 2D, 3D trigonometry intro, linear programming, discrete mathematics (network pathways, Eulerian paths intro).

### myp-biology-y4/y5, myp-chemistry-y4/y5, myp-physics-y4/y5
Prefixes: `mypb4-`, `mypb5-`, `mypc4-`, `mypc5-`, `mypp4-`, `mypp5-`. Three units per course following each subject's topic areas (MYP sciences key concepts Change, Relationships, Systems; criteria A-D as before). Year 5 deepens Year 4.
- Biology unit-1 "Cell Biology and Classification": cell structure and function, microscopy and magnification, classification and evolutionary change, variation and adaptation.
- Biology unit-2 "Form and Function": tissues, organs and organ systems (plants and humans), homeostasis and feedback, reproduction and inheritance basics, disease and immunity.
- Biology unit-3 "Interaction and Interdependence / Ecology": ecosystems, energy flow and food webs, nutrient cycles, populations, human impact and conservation.
- Chemistry unit-1 "Particles and Atomic Structure": particle model, atomic structure, the periodic table, bonding (ionic, covalent, metallic) intro.
- Chemistry unit-2 "Chemical Reactions and Energetics": reaction types, balancing and stoichiometry intro, acids/bases and salts, rates of reaction, energy changes.
- Chemistry unit-3 "Materials, Organic Chemistry and Earth": materials and their uses, organic chemistry intro (hydrocarbons, alcohols), the atmosphere and climate chemistry, chemistry of the Earth.
- Physics unit-1 "Motion and Forces": kinematics graphs extended, Newton's laws intro, momentum, pressure, moments.
- Physics unit-2 "Energy and Waves": energy stores/transfers/efficiency, thermal physics, wave properties, sound and light, the EM spectrum.
- Physics unit-3 "Electricity, Magnetism and Space": circuits, Ohm's law, electromagnetism, static electricity, astronomy and space physics.
